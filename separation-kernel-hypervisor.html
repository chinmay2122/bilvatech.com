02 / ARCHITECTURECONTEXT & CAPABILITIES

THE FOUNDATION

Defined boundaries for complex computing environments.

Controlled access to CPU, memory, I/O, peripherals and communication resources creates a structured foundation for greater security, predictability, fault containment and resource efficiency.

Different workloads can share the platform without sharing unrestricted access.
