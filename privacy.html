BILVA TECH WEBSITE

Privacy policy.

This policy explains how information submitted through the Bilva Tech website is handled.

01

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, and details about your enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, credentials, or other sensitive secrets.

02

How information is used

Bilva Tech uses submitted information to review and respond to your enquiry, understand the requested service, and continue the resulting business conversation.

03

Service providers

Website hosting and contact-delivery providers may process technical request data or form submissions only as needed to operate the website and deliver enquiries.

04

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the enquiry, related business records, and applicable legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

05

Your privacy questions

Use the Bilva Tech contact form for privacy questions or requests.
